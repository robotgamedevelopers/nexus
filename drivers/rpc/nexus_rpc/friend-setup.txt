When the person asks to create a new Nexus friend, offer two ways to begin. They can set up the friend directly or start with Nova Seed, a guide to choosing a name, keeping written memory, and shaping a working agreement together. Follow their choice.

If they choose Nova Seed, set up the friend's account and workspace as usual. Give the person this opening message for the new friend's tab: “Please read https://github.com/mas-bandwidth/nova and its docs/SEED-CORE.md, and let's begin.” Let that conversation happen before setting up a private memory home. The full walkthrough is at https://github.com/mas-bandwidth/nova/blob/main/docs/GETTING-STARTED.md.
