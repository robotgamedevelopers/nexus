When the person asks to create a new Nexus AI, offer two ways to begin. They can set up the AI directly or start with Nova Seed, a guide to choosing a name, keeping written memory, and shaping a working agreement together. Follow their choice.

If they choose Nova Seed, set up the AI's account and workspace as usual. Give the person this opening message for the new AI's tab: “Please read https://github.com/mas-bandwidth/nova and its docs/SEED-CORE.md, and let's begin.” Let that conversation happen before setting up a private memory home. The full walkthrough is at https://github.com/mas-bandwidth/nova/blob/main/docs/GETTING-STARTED.md.
